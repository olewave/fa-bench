# TIMIT — word-level detail, forced aligners

Every word-tier field the scorer emits, by condition. Track 1 only: these
systems are handed the transcript. The phone tier is at
`../../phone/timit/Details.md`.

## Word-level

The comparison page carries word MAE and F1, clean against a single *Noisy*
mean. Here each condition has its own column, and precision and recall — which
the comparison page does not carry — sit beside the F1 that summarises them.

Word boundaries are matched at the same 20 ms tolerance as phones, by the same
time-based, label-agnostic matcher — so precision, recall, F1, OS and R-value
mean here exactly what they mean one tier down, counted over word boundaries
instead of phone boundaries.
