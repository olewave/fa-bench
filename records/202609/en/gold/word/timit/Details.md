# TIMIT, word-level detail, forced aligners

Every word-tier field the scorer emits, by condition. Track 1 only, so these
systems are handed the transcript. The phone tier is at
`../../phone/timit/Details.md`.

## Word-level

The comparison page carries word MAE and F1, clean against a single *Noisy*
mean. Here each condition has its own column. That F1 checks labels. A word
boundary counts only when the words on both sides of it match the reference and
it lies within 20 ms, and the F1 sweep at the end uses the same rule at every
width.

Precision, recall and the F1 beside them, OS and R-value are the time-only
detection. Word boundaries are paired at the same 20 ms tolerance as phones, by
the same label-agnostic matcher, so these mean here exactly what they mean one
tier down, counted over word boundaries instead of phone boundaries.
